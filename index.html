The brief

Make the value obvious in five seconds.

Nexora's buyers are owners and ops leads at teams of 2 to 50 people. They don't have time to read about "workflow orchestration". They want to know what gets taken off their plate and what it costs.

The brief covered:

  • Landing page design for desktop and mobile
  • Pricing, features, testimonials, and FAQ sections
  • Interactive prototype and design handoff
Key decisions

Show the product, then remove doubts.

Product as the hero

A real dashboard sits next to the headline, showing hours saved and live automation runs. Visitors see the outcome before they read about it.

Benefits in plain words

"Get home on time" instead of "Optimize operations". Every feature is written as a task it takes off your list.

One call to action

Every button leads to the same 20-minute demo form, which asks for four fields and nothing more.

Violet glass identity

Deep violet surfaces, frosted panels, and soft 3D objects that respond to the cursor give a calm, premium feel.

Mobile

Built for the phone first scroll.

Single column, a full-screen menu, and the demo form within easy thumb reach.

Page structure

Built around the questions buyers ask.

  • Hero: what it is, who it's for, and proof in the dashboard
  • Features: automations, tasks, and integrations with real examples
  • How it works: three steps to show setup takes an afternoon
  • Testimonials: quotes with a measurable before-and-after
  • Pricing: three plans with a monthly and yearly switch
  • FAQ: answers to setup, data, and cancellation worries
  • Demo booking: a short form with a clear next step
