FAQ

Questions teams ask before the demo.

Something else on your mind? Ask us on the demo call.

Do we need a developer to set it up?

No. Automations are built from plain steps like "when an invoice is overdue, send a reminder". Most teams are running within one afternoon.

Can we move our data from another tool?

Yes. We import from Trello, Asana, Monday, and CSV files. On Growth and Scale we'll do the import for you.

Where is our data stored?

In EU data centres in Frankfurt and Dublin, encrypted at rest and in transit, with daily backups. We're GDPR compliant.

What happens after the free trial?

You choose a plan or your workspace pauses. Nothing is charged automatically, and your data stays available for 30 days.

Can we cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month. Yearly plans can be refunded pro rata in the first 60 days.
